Fluffy Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp melted butter

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. Whisk milk, egg, and melted butter in another bowl.
  3. Combine wet and dry ingredients until just mixed.
  4. Heat a pan and pour batter to form pancakes.
  5. Cook until golden on both sides and serve with syrup.
