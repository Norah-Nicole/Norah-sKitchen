Oats with Mango & Banana

Prep: 5 min Cook: 7 min Serves: 2

Creamy oats topped with fresh tropical fruit makes a light, healthy breakfast that’s both filling and refreshing.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup oats
  • 2 cups milk or water
  • 1 ripe mango, diced
  • 1 ripe banana, sliced
  • 1 tbsp honey

Steps

  1. Bring milk to a gentle boil.
  2. Add oats and cook on low for 5–7 minutes.
  3. Let cool slightly.
  4. Serve and top with mango and banana.
  5. Drizzle with honey if desired.

Serving Suggestion

Serve chilled for a refreshing start, or warm for comfort.
