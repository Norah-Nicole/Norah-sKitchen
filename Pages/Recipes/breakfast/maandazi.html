Maandazi

Prep: 40 min Cook: 30 min Serves: 5

soft, golden and slightly sweet maandazi. Fluffy on the inside and lightly crisp on the outside.

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • Self raising flour
  • margarine of choice
  • sugar
  • Milk
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • Cinnamon(optional)

Steps

  1. In a bowl, mix flour,sugar and cinnamon(if using).
  2. Add margarine and rub into the flour until crumbly.
  3. Beat the eggs,add them to the mixture,then pour in the milk gradually.
  4. add the 3tbs oil and knead into a smoft, smooth dough.
  5. cover the dough and let it rest for 20 minutes
  6. roll out the dough on a flat surface and cut into triangles or desired shape
  7. heat oil in a deep pan and fry the pieces until golden brown on both sides
  8. Drain on paper towels and serve warm.

Serving Suggestion

Pair with Kenyan chai for a classic morning combination.
