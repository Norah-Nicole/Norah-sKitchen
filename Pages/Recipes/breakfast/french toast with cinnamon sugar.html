French Toast with Cinnamon Sugar

Prep: 5 min Cook: 10 min Serves: 2

Golden, fluffy, and lightly spiced with cinnamon — this French toast is the perfect cozy breakfast that feels indulgent yet simple.

Ingredients

  • 4 slices of bread
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence
  • Butter or oil for frying
  • Extra sugar and cinnamon for dusting

Steps

  1. Whisk eggs, milk, sugar, cinnamon, and vanilla in a shallow bowl.
  2. Heat a pan and melt a little butter.
  3. Dip each bread slice to coat both sides.
  4. Fry on medium heat until golden on both sides.
  5. Remove and sprinkle with cinnamon sugar.

Serving Suggestion

Serve warm with honey, syrup, or fresh fruit on the side.
