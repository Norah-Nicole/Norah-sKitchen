Spanish Omelette

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 5 eggs
  • Olive oil
  • Salt & pepper

Instructions

  1. Peel and slice the potatoes and onion.
  2. Fry them in olive oil until soft.
  3. Beat eggs in a bowl and season with salt & pepper.
  4. Mix potatoes and onion with eggs.
  5. Cook in a pan until firm, flipping once.
