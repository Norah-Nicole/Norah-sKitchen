Chapati

Prep: 15 min Rest: 30 min Cook: 20 min Makes: 6–8

Soft, flaky, and golden — chapati is the ultimate Kenyan classic, loved at breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp oil or melted butter
  • ¾ cup warm water
  • Extra flour for rolling
  • Oil for frying

Steps

  1. Mix flour, salt, sugar, and oil.
  2. Add warm water and knead to a soft dough.
  3. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
  4. Divide into balls and roll into circles.
  5. Cook on a hot pan until golden spots appear, then flip.
  6. Brush lightly with oil on both sides.
  7. Repeat with remaining dough.

Serving Suggestion

Serve hot with chai, stew, or even plain with avocado.
