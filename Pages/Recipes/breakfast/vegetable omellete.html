Vegetable Omelette

Prep: 10 min Cook: 10 min Serves: 2

A hearty vegetable omelette creating the perfect balance of savory and sweet.

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • ½ onion, finely chopped
  • ½ green pepper, diced
  • 1 small carrot, grated
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Beat eggs and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Stir in the chopped vegetables.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and pour in the mixture.
  4. Cook until set, flip, and cook until golden.
  5. Serve hot.

Serving Suggestion

Pair with Kenyan chai for a classic morning combination.
