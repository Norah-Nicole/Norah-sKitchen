Egg Sandwich

Prep: 5 min Cook: 10 min Serves: 2

Quick, filling, and protein-packed — the egg sandwich is the perfect grab-and-go Kenyan breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 4 slices of bread
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil or butter
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh coriander, optional

Steps

  1. Beat eggs with salt, pepper, onion, tomato, and coriander.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and cook until set, flipping once.
  3. Cut the omelette to fit the bread.
  4. Place between bread slices.
  5. Toast or press the sandwich if desired.

Serving Suggestion

Enjoy with a cup of chai or fresh juice. Add avocado for extra creaminess.
