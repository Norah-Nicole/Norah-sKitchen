Millet Uji with Honey

Prep: 5 min Cook: 12 min Serves: 2

Nutritious, wholesome, and comforting, millet uji is a traditional Kenyan porridge with a modern twist of honey sweetness.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup millet flour
  • 3 cups water
  • 1 cup milk
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 tbsp honey

Steps

  1. Mix millet flour with 1 cup water to form a slurry.
  2. Bring 2 cups water to a boil.
  3. Slowly add the slurry while stirring.
  4. Cook on low heat for 8–10 minutes, stirring often.
  5. Add milk and salt and simmer 3 minutes.
  6. Remove from heat and stir in honey.

Serving Suggestion

Enjoy hot with groundnuts or a slice of sweet potato.
