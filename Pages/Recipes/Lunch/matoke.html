Matoke

Prep: 15 mins Cook: 30–40 mins Serves: 4

Introduction

Soft, tender green bananas simmered with vegetables and spices — a wholesome dish from Western Kenya.

Ingredients

  • 8 green bananas, peeled & sliced
  • 2 medium potatoes, diced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, blended
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 cup water or broth
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander

Instructions

  1. Heat oil, fry onions until soft.
  2. Add turmeric and stir in tomatoes.
  3. Add bananas, potatoes, and carrots. Stir well.
  4. Pour in water/broth, season with salt.
  5. Cover and cook until bananas are tender.
  6. Garnish with coriander before serving.

Serving Suggestion

Enjoy with chapati or on its own as a filling meal.
