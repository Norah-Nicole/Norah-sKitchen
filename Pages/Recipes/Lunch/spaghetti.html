Spaghetti

Prep: 10 mins Cook: 15 mins Serves: 4

Simple yet satisfying — spaghetti tossed in a rich tomato sauce makes an easy Kenyan lunch favorite.

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tomatoes, blended
  • 1 tsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp oregano/basil (optional)
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Grated cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Boil spaghetti in salted water until al dente, drain.
  2. Heat oil, sauté onion and garlic.
  3. Add tomatoes and paste, cook into sauce.
  4. Season with salt, pepper, and herbs.
  5. Toss spaghetti in sauce until coated.

Serving Suggestion

Top with grated cheese and serve with a side of salad.
