Githeri and Potato Stew

Prep: 10 mins Cook: 25–30 mins Serves: 4

Introduction

A classic Kenyan comfort dish — githeri (maize and beans) cooked with potatoes in a rich stew.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pre-boiled githeri (maize & beans)
  • 2 medium potatoes, cubed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, blended
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander

Instructions

  1. Heat oil and sauté onions until soft.
  2. Add curry powder, paprika, and stir.
  3. Add tomatoes and cook into a sauce.
  4. Stir in potatoes and carrots, add 1 cup water, cook until soft.
  5. Add githeri, simmer for 10 minutes, garnish with coriander.

Serving Suggestion

Serve hot with chapati or avocado on the side.
