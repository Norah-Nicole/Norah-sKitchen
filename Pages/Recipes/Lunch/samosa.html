Samosa

Prep: 40 mins(including resting dough) Cook: 15-20 mins Serves: 10-12

Introduction

Crispy, golden pastries stuffed with spiced meat or vegetables — a beloved Kenyan snack that doubles as a light lunch.

Ingredients

Filling

  • 250g minced beef/chicken OR mixed vegetables
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ginger, grated
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Pastry

  • 2 cups flour
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • ½ cup warm water

Instructions

  1. Prepare pastry dough with flour, salt, oil, and water. Let rest 30 mins.
  2. Cook filling: sauté onion, garlic, ginger, spices, then add meat/veg. Cook until dry.
  3. Roll dough thin, cut into strips, fold into cones, and fill. Seal with flour paste.
  4. Deep fry until golden and crispy.

Serving Suggestion

Serve with chili sauce or ketchup.
