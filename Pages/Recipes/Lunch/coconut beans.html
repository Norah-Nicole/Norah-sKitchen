Coconut Beans

Prep: 10 mins Cook: 15 mins Serves: 4

Introduction

Creamy, rich, and comforting — coconut beans are a Kenyan coastal-inspired dish that pairs beautifully with rice or chapati.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups boiled beans (red, yellow, or mixed)
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, blended or grated
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ginger, grated
  • 1 green chili (optional)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a pan and sauté onions until soft.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, and chili, stir until fragrant.
  3. Stir in tomatoes and cook until thick.
  4. Add boiled beans, mix well.
  5. Pour in coconut milk, season with salt, and simmer for 10 minutes.

Serving Suggestion

Serve hot with steamed rice, chapati, or ugali.
