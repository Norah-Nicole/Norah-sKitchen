Seasoned fries

Prep: 40 mins(including soaking) Cook: 15-20 mins Serves: 4

Introduction

Crispy golden fries tossed in Kenyan-style spices — a perfect side or light lunch.

Ingredients

  • 4 large potatoes, peeled & cut into fries
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp chili powder
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Soak fries in water for 30 minutes, pat dry.
  2. Heat oil in a pan, fry until golden and crispy.
  3. Toss hot fries in paprika, chili, garlic powder, and salt.

Serving Suggestion

Serve with ketchup, chili sauce, or alongside nyama choma.
