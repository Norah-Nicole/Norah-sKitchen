Ndengu curry

Prep: 10 min Cook: 15 min Serves: 4

Introduction

Green grams (ndengu) cooked in aromatic spices make a hearty curry that’s both healthy and flavorful.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups boiled ndengu (green grams)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, blended
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ginger, grated
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Instructions

  1. Heat oil and fry onions until golden.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, curry powder, and turmeric. Stir briefly.
  3. Add tomatoes and cook into a thick paste.
  4. Stir in ndengu and simmer with 1 cup water for 10 minutes.
  5. Adjust seasoning, garnish with coriander.

Serving Suggestion

Enjoy with chapati or steamed rice.
