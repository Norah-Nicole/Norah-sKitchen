Egg Fried Rice

Prep: 10 mins Cook: 10 mins Serves: 4

Introduction

Colorful, flavorful rice stir-fried with eggs and vegetables — a quick and tasty one-pan meal.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked rice (preferably cold)
  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Spring onions for garnish

Instructions

  1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok, scramble eggs, set aside.
  2. Add remaining oil, fry onions and vegetables.
  3. Stir in rice and soy sauce, mix well.
  4. Add scrambled eggs, season, and toss everything together.

Serving Suggestion

Serve with grilled chicken or as a standalone lunch.
