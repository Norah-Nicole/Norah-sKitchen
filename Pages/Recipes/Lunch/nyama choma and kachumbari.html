Prep: 1 hour (including marinating) Cook: 20–30 mins Serves: 4

Introduction

Kenya’s most famous dish — smoky, char-grilled meat paired with fresh, zesty kachumbari.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg goat or beef, cubed
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 1 tbsp black pepper
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tbsp oil

For Kachumbari

  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 onion, finely sliced
  • 1 chili, chopped (optional)
  • Coriander leaves, chopped
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Marinate meat with salt, pepper, lemon juice, and oil for 1 hour.
  2. Grill over charcoal until cooked and smoky.
  3. Mix all kachumbari ingredients in a bowl.

Serving Suggestion

Serve nyama choma hot with ugali or fries, plus fresh kachumbari on the side.
