Prep: 10 mins Cook: 15 mins Serves: 4

Introduction

Nutritious, protein-rich lentils simmered with spices make a comforting and healthy meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups boiled lentils
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, blended
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (optional)
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander

Instructions

  1. Heat oil, add cumin seeds until they pop.
  2. Add onions, garlic, and fry until soft.
  3. Stir in curry powder and tomatoes, cook into a paste.
  4. Add lentils with 1 cup water, simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Garnish with coriander before serving.

Serving Suggestion

Pair with chapati, rice, or flatbread.
