Potato Wedges

Prep: 10 min Cook: 30 min Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Oil
  • Paprika, garlic powder, salt

Steps

  1. Toss wedges with oil and seasoning.
  2. Bake until crisp and tender.
