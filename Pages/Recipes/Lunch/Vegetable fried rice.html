Vegetable Fried Rice

Prep: 10 min Cook: 15 min Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked rice
  • Mixed vegetables
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Spring onions

Steps

  1. Sauté garlic and vegetables.
  2. Add rice and soy sauce and stir-fry.
  3. Finish with spring onions.
