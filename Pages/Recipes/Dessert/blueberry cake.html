Blueberry Cake

Prep: 15 min Cook: 35 min Serves: 8

Ingredients

  • Flour, sugar, baking powder
  • Milk, eggs, butter
  • Blueberries

Steps

  1. Make batter and fold in blueberries.
  2. Bake until golden and set.
