Mango & Passion Fruit Sorbet

Prep: 10 min Freeze: 3 hrs Serves: 6

Ingredients

  • Mango pulp
  • Passion fruit pulp
  • Sugar or honey
  • Lime juice

Steps

  1. Blend all ingredients until smooth.
  2. Freeze and churn or stir periodically.
