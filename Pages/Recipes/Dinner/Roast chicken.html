Roast Chicken

Prep: 15 min Cook: 60 min Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Whole chicken
  • Garlic, lemon, herbs
  • Salt, pepper, oil

Steps

  1. Season chicken inside and out.
  2. Roast until juices run clear.
