Chicken Pilau

Prep: 15 min Cook: 40 min Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces
  • Rice
  • Pilau masala, onions, garlic

Steps

  1. Sauté onions with spices.
  2. Add chicken, rice, water and cook.
